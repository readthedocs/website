{{ about_divide("Business FAQ") }}

What happens when the trial ends?

We won't charge you automatically — you only pay if you decide to continue.

Is my documentation indexed during the trial?

During the trial period, your documentation is served with a noindex robots directive, so search engines won't index it. Subscribe to a paid plan to make your documentation indexable.

Do you offer annual pricing or purchase orders?

Yes. Annual plans are available on every paid tier and can be paid by invoice or purchase order.

What about open source projects?

Open source documentation is always free on Read the Docs Community.
